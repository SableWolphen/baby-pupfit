🗓️ 12-week roadmap

  1. Weeks 1–2: Establish food, steps and machine baselines. Log accurately and avoid rushing weights.
  2. Weeks 3–6: Progress hip thrust, leg press and weighted ab crunches while maintaining moderate fat loss.
  3. Weeks 7–10: Compare waist and seven-day weight averages, plus your training performance. Adjust only if needed.
  4. Weeks 11–12: Review results. Maintain, continue, or take a recovery-focused week as needed.

A loss of roughly 0.5–1 lb weekly may be reasonable if you maintain performance; 163–169 lb after 12 weeks is a possible range, not a required weight or guarantee of visible abs.
