📈 Progression rule

Most sets should finish with about 1–3 clean reps still possible. When every set reaches the top of its rep range with good form and roughly 1–2 reps left, increase the machine by the smallest sensible increment next time. Usually that is about 5 lb, or about 2.5 lb on smaller movements when available.

🍼 Tiny-mode priority

If time is awful, do the highest-return three for that lower-body day: Glute Drive → Leg Press → Hip Abduction or Kickback. A short focused session beats skipping.

🥩 Body-composition basics

Keep the main tracker targets: roughly 1,900–2,100 calories, 150g+ protein, and at least 7,000 steps as the starting setup. The waist gets leaner from the calorie/step side; the bubble butt and thighs come from progressive machine training.
