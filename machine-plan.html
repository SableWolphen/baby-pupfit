No heavy leg press, hack squat, or heavy hamstring work Friday. Saturday restores your legs before the Sunday run.

Saturday • 😴 Full rest

Easy walking and normal mobility are fine. Save your recovery for Sunday and the next week.

Sunday • 🏃 3–5 mile run

Keep most long runs easy/moderate rather than racing every week. Monday is upper body so your legs get a full day before Tuesday's heavy lower-body session.

📈 Progression rule

Most working sets: 1–2 RIR. If a prescription is 3 × 8–12, keep the same load until you can do 12 / 12 / 12 cleanly with roughly 1–2 reps left, then use the smallest useful weight increase.

Do not add extra hard sets just to speed things up. Faster progress comes from better recovery + consistent progression + nutrition, not burying yourself in junk volume.

⚡ Fast-change checklist

High protein, a moderate calorie deficit while leaning out, 8k–12k daily steps if recovery is good, 3–5 g/day creatine monohydrate if you tolerate it, and 7.5–9 hours of sleep. The reference art exaggerates anatomy, so the target is the closest realistic silhouette for your frame.
