📈 Progressive overload + ✨ ab progression

Abs are a priority. On Wednesday and Saturday, treat the Ab Crunch Machine like a main lift: 4 × 10–15, controlled reps, full range, and progressive resistance. Pick a load that leaves about 1–3 good reps in reserve. Stay inside the prescribed rep range. Once every prescribed set reaches the top of the range with clean form, increase resistance by the smallest useful amount.

Example: 70 × 10 → 70 × 12 → 75 × 9 → 75 × 12. The tracker remembers your last session and gives you the next goal.

No daily body measurements. Use workout performance daily, with waist/weight once weekly if you want them.

🐶 What the tracker should show

For every exercise: Today’s Workout → exercise → sets × reps → current weight → last performance → next goal → Start Exercise. After each set, tap Too heavy / Just right / Easy. The app uses that feedback plus completed reps to adjust future suggestions.
