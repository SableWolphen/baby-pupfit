🎯 Priority order

This plan is built around your exact visual priority: 1) glutes, 2) abs, 3) arms/upper body, 4) thighs. Thigh work stays in because it supports glute growth and proportion, but it no longer drives the program.

1. 🍑 Glute Drive / Hip Thrust
Primary projection and glute-strength builder.

Builds an athletic V-taper and makes the waist look smaller.

Visible arm shape with low recovery cost.

Keeps thighs proportional and supports the glute goal without dominating volume.

📈 Fast-progress rule

Most work sets: 1–2 RIR. When every prescribed set reaches the top of its rep range with clean form, use the smallest available weight increase next time. If performance drops for two sessions, hold or reduce load rather than adding junk volume.

Prioritize progression in this order: Hip Thrust → Ab Crunch → Pulldown/Row → Arms → supportive thigh lifts.

✨ Abs are non-negotiable

Train them like a muscle, not like cardio. Add reps and resistance over time. The program gives you direct ab work three times per week, while getting leaner is what reveals the definition.

⏱️ Tiny Mode • 25–35 min

Glute day: Hip Thrust → Abduction → Ab Crunch → Knee Raise. Upper day: Pulldown → Row → Curl → Pressdown. This preserves your actual priorities when time is tight.

Exact results vary with anatomy, training history, nutrition, recovery, and body-fat level. Use pain-free ranges and adjust machines to fit you.
