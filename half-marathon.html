🐾 Your half-marathon path

Train toward 13.1 miles while keeping your glute, shoulder and ab workouts. Your long run is on Sunday; weekdays are short enough to fit before work.

Start from your real running base

Tuesday
20–30 min easy after lifting only if recovered; otherwise separate session
Friday
20–35 min easy after upper day or another convenient time
Sunday
Gradually increasing long easy run; run/walk is fine
Wednesday
Optional 15–25 min recovery jog instead of extra lifting

Keep Thursday glute day. If Tuesday's leg session leaves you sore, move the easy run to Wednesday. Never force mileage on painful legs or cut your sleep to squeeze in a run.

Long-run progression

This is a cautious illustrative build, not a race-date guarantee. Weekly totals matter too; avoid a long run that dwarfs your other mileage. Adjust based on your last 3–4 weeks of running and recovery.

Fuel your runs

Don't try to race-train on dinner alone. Aim for regular meals and your established daily protein goal. Before longer runs, have tolerable carbohydrate; for runs beyond about 75–90 minutes, practice carrying fluids and carbohydrates. Hydration depends on heat and sweat rate, especially in Texas.

Mostly run at a conversational pace. If shin, foot, knee or hip pain persists, reduce mileage and seek qualified assessment. Keep a full rest day.
