🐾 Your gym buddy

Build a real reminder routine. A reminder in your phone calendar works more reliably than one that depends on a web page staying open.

1. Plan your before-work workout

Suggested start: 5:00 AM to leave room to drive home, shower privately, and get to work by 8:00 AM. Adjust to your real travel times.

Downloads an .ics calendar file. Open the file and import the workouts to your phone's calendar. Check that notifications are enabled. Events include 60-minute and 15-minute alerts if your calendar supports them.

Suggested morning timeline

5:00–5:55 AM gym · 5:55–6:15 AM drive home (estimate) · 6:15–7:00 AM shower and get ready at home · 7:00–8:00 AM commute and buffer. Adjust for your actual drives.

2. Make showing up easy

The tiny-win rule: If motivation disappears, go for just 10 minutes. Warm up, do one machine, and decide whether to continue. No punishment workouts and no catching up on missed sets.

Before bed, put your shoes and gym clothes where you can't miss them. When the first reminder goes off, start getting ready rather than deciding whether to exercise.

3. Keep the streak realistic

Monday upper + abs · Tuesday glutes · Wednesday optional · Thursday glutes · Friday upper · Saturday rest · Sunday easy run.

Missed a day? Just return at the next planned session. Rest is part of progress.
