# Baby PupFit • 6-week curvier-body sprint

**Goal:** develop fuller glutes/thighs and a more defined waist as quickly as training and recovery safely allow. A dramatic new body or visible six-pack in six weeks is **not guaranteed**. Do not use estrogen, testosterone blockers, SARMs, fat burners, or extreme dieting as shortcuts—particularly because preserving ejaculation is a priority.

**Before lifting:** 5–10 minutes of easy movement, then lighter warm-up sets for first big lift. Most working sets should finish with **1–3 reps in reserve**; no need for failure. If the session doesn't fit your morning window, remove the final accessory—not warm-up or recovery.

## The six-week progression
- **Week 1 • Baseline:** log machine settings, weights and actual reps. Do not chase maximums. Measure waist at navel and hips/glutes at their widest point once; use consistent morning photos if you want.
- **Week 2 • Repeatability:** aim for 1 extra controlled rep on some working sets, or improved range of motion, at the same weights.
- **Week 3 • Small increases:** only when **every** set hits the top of its prescribed rep range with good form and about 2 reps left, increase weight by the smallest machine increment next session.
- **Week 4 • Quality growth:** continue the same sets; prioritize glutes and avoid adding junk volume. Keep runs easy and follow your run-plan progression.
- **Week 5 • Recovery check:** if performance is improving and you feel good, continue; if persistent soreness or run fatigue affects form, remove a set from each lower-body exercise or use a lighter week.
- **Week 6 • Compare:** repeat waist/hip measures, strength log and consistent photos. Plan the next block from real progress rather than a promised target size.

**Progression rule:** Increase *reps first*, then load. If you miss the bottom of a rep range, reduce load or rest longer. If you feel joint pain, stop the exercise and substitute safely. No make-up sessions.

## Eat to create curves without losing muscle
- Aim for **130–160 g protein/day** spread over 3–4 easy meals or snacks.
- **Don't skip lunch.** Simple packed options: Greek yogurt + sandwich; microwave rice/chicken bowl; protein shake and a substantial meal.
- Eat carbohydrates around runs and lifting. For runs exceeding ~75–90 minutes, practice taking **30–60 g carbohydrates per hour** as tolerated and fluids appropriate to heat/sweat.
- Do **not** crash diet while also building glutes and increasing running mileage. If waist-fat reduction is desired, use a small calorie deficit only after observing a stable baseline and checking energy and progress for 2–3 weeks.
- **Optional supplements:** plain creatine monohydrate 3–5 g/day if medically appropriate; whey/plant protein only to fill dietary gaps. Creatine may increase scale weight through muscle water. No proven supplement selectively moves fat to hips or preserves semen volume during hormone therapy.

## Weekday consistency checklist

- [ ] Lay out workout clothes, water bottle and gym bag
- [ ] Get enough sleep (7–9 hours); an early gym trip isn't worth chronic sleep loss
- [ ] Complete the scheduled session **or** take recovery when needed
- [ ] Record actual sets/reps; check in with energy and soreness
- [ ] Eat after training, including protein and carbohydrate

## Week 6 scorecard
Record baseline and week-6 **waist**, **hip/glute circumference**, **hip thrust reps/weight**, **leg press reps/weight**, **longest comfortable easy run** and **recovery/energy**. Individual measurements and visual changes may be small over six weeks.

**Reproductive health note:** Because ejaculation volume/force has been decreasing gradually for years, arrange a nonurgent urology evaluation. Avoid experimenting with hormones or hormone-altering supplements to accelerate feminization if maintaining ejaculation matters.
