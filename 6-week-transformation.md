# Baby PupFit: accelerated 6-week checkpoint

**Baseline:** 178 lb, 34-inch waist, 5'8"; reported prior training 5+ days weekly. The goal is early visible improvements in muscle definition and strength, not a guaranteed six-pack in six weeks.

## Weekly training (machine/cable only)
- **Monday — Upper A + abs:** incline chest press, ISO-lateral pulldown, cable lateral raise, EGYM row, ab crunch machine. Keep workout within 45–55 minutes.
- **Tuesday — Glutes + thighs + abs:** Booty Builder hip thrust, EGYM leg press, EGYM leg curl, EGYM leg extension, hip abduction, ab crunch.
- **Wednesday — OPTIONAL:** light rear-delts, lateral raise, EGYM biceps, calves, or rest.
- **Thursday — Glutes + thighs + abs:** hip thrust, leg press, leg curl, leg extension, abduction, ab crunch.
- **Friday — Upper B + abs:** EGYM chest press, shoulder press, cable lateral raise, pec deck fly, pulldown, row, ab crunch.
- **Saturday:** rest.
- **Sunday:** conversational 3–5-mile run, shortened if needed for recovery.

The exact exercises, sets, and rep ranges are loaded in the app's Plan/Workout screens. The existing first-eight-session gradual-volume safeguard and machine-calibration guidance still apply. Existing logged sessions are preserved and are **not** retroactively rewritten.

## Training progression
- **Week 1:** establish clean baseline loads while training close to, but not at, failure (typically 2–3 reps in reserve).
- **Week 2:** improve reps or technique where possible; full scheduled workouts only when recovery is adequate.
- **Weeks 3–4:** progress individual exercise loads only after all prescribed sets reach the top of their rep range with ~2 reps in reserve. Log real data.
- **Weeks 5–6:** maintain progressive work; if fatigue, pain, or declining performance persists, reduce volume and prioritize recovery. Measure results at the end of week 6.

No make-up sets for missed workouts, and no guaranteed abdominal visibility. A 34-inch waist cannot establish body-fat percentage. Spot fat reduction does not work.

## Nutrition and measurement
Start with **130–160 g protein/day**. For desired gradual fat loss, consider a modest ~250–400 kcal/day deficit *relative to observed maintenance*, not a fixed calorie prescription. Track 3–7 morning weigh-ins per week and compare weekly averages; measure waist at the navel weekly under consistent conditions. A possible rate is roughly 0.5–1 lb per week if the weight trend supports it. Adjust only after 2–3 weeks. Aggressive deficits risk compromising training, energy, and muscle fullness.

## Workday logistics
Sample only, **not verified drive times**:
4:30 wake → 5:00–5:55 gym → drive **home** for a private shower → get ready → leave for 8:00 work. Shorten workouts if driving requires it, never sacrifice sleep. Aim for 7–9 hours/night; 4:30 wake implies bedtime around 8:30–9:30 PM.

## Safety
Warm up 5–10 minutes; use machines as calibrated (especially EGYM). Leave 1–3 reps in reserve on most sets, stop if pain arises, and rest enough between muscle groups. Rapid transformation promises are not medically sound.
