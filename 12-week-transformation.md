# Baby PupFit — 12-week accelerated, machine-only plan

Starting measurements (self-reported October 2026): 5'8", 178 lb, waist 34". Training history: 5+ sessions/week. Aim: visible midsection definition plus full glutes/thighs and rounded shoulders, preserving recovery.

## Morning plan (illustrative; confirm drive times)
4:30 AM wake; 5:00–5:55 train at EOS K Avenue; 5:55–6:15 drive HOME; 6:15–7:00 shower/get ready AT HOME; 7:00–8:00 commute/buffer to 8 AM work. If travel takes longer, shorten training or move session; do not reduce sleep below 7 hours.

## Weekly sessions (45–55 minutes including 5–7 minute warm-up)
**Monday Upper A + abs:** Incline chest press 3x8–12; lat pulldown 3x8–12; shoulder press 2x8–12; lateral raise cable 3x12–20; machine abdominal crunch 3x10–15. 
**Tuesday Glutes/legs A:** Hip thrust machine 3x8–12; leg press 3x8–12; seated/lying leg curl 3x10–15; hip abduction machine 2x12–20; leg extension 2x10–15.
**Wednesday Optional easy detail:** rear delt machine 2x12–20; EGYM biceps 2x10–15; calf machine 2x12–20; easy cardio 10–15 min. Skip if fatigued.
**Thursday Glutes/legs B + abs:** Hip thrust machine 3x10–15; leg press 3x10–15; leg curl 3x10–15; hip abduction 3x12–20; machine ab crunch 3x10–15.
**Friday Upper B + abs:** EGYM chest press 3x8–12; machine row 3x8–12; cable lateral raise 3x12–20; pec fly 2x10–15; EGYM triceps press 2x10–15; ab crunch machine 2x10–15.
**Saturday** rest. **Sunday** conversational 3–5 mile run, optional shorter/easier for recovery.

Warm up first. Rest 90–150 sec on major lifts and 60–90 sec for isolation, adjusting workout duration. For EGYM Smart Strength, use its calibrated resistance guidance rather than manually overriding.

## Progression and safety
Week 1–2: full planned schedule if prior training supports it, but begin with 1–3 reps in reserve and reduce sets if recovering poorly. Weeks 3–6: add reps before small weight increments; increase weight only when all sets reach top range with good form and ~2 reps in reserve. Weeks 7–10: maintain challenging volume if recovered. Week 11–12: maintain, assess trends, optionally ease loads/sets in response to fatigue. Do not train through pain; no forced failure, extreme cardio, or make-up sessions. Minimum 7 hours nightly sleep, preferably 7–9.

## Nutrition
Protein starting range: **130–160 g/day** (about 1.6–2.0 g/kg). Track average weight on 3–7 mornings/week (not single-day fluctuations), waist once weekly, and exercise strength. If fat loss is appropriate and desired, choose a modest energy deficit, initially around 250–400 kcal/day below observed maintenance, adjusted after 2–3 weeks. Avoid crash dieting. Aim at most about 0.5–1.0 lb/week while prioritizing muscle retention; faster is not inherently better for glutes/strength. No exact calories without actual intake and trend data. Adequate dietary fat, carbs and hydration support training.

## Realistic ab goal
At 34-inch waist and 178 lb, body-fat percentage cannot be accurately inferred. A six-pack depends on abdominal muscle development, fat distribution and genetics. 8–12 weeks can improve definition but cannot guarantee a six-pack; artistic/anime figures are exaggerated. No spot reduction. Favor trend measurements, not daily comparisons.
