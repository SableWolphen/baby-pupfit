💪 How progression works

Baby PupFit starts you with a prescribed weight. After you build history, it automatically uses your previous performance to decide whether to keep the weight or increase it.
Reference-physique direction
🎯 Lean waist + abs + round projected glutes + thick thighs
This is the direction of travel, not a promise of identical proportions. Bone structure and fat distribution vary.
Waist↓ smaller / tighter
Abs↑ more visible
Glute projection↑ farther back
Upper-glute shelf↑ fuller / higher
Thighs↑ fuller / thicker
Upper body→ athletic, maintain
